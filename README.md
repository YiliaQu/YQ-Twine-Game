
This is an interactive novel/game I created. Supported by NYU DH Graduate Fellowship. 
Originally developed and published in the Summer of 2024. Minor style change was made in the Summer of 2026.

This game is designed to be an experimental alternative to mainstream Parenting Simulation Games. 
This game is part of *Without Truth*, a series of creative projects that explores the topic of family, diaspora, queerness, non-linear narrative, and memory.

## How to Play
1. Download the HTML file.
2. Open it in your web browser.


Feel free to email me (yilia.yijia.qu@gmail.com) to give feedback or suggestions.

Thank you for playing, enjoy!
